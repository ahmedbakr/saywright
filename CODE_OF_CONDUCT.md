# Code of conduct

Saywright follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and patient, welcome people of every background and level of experience, give and take feedback gracefully, and focus on what is best for the people who use and build Saywright. Harassment, insults and personal or political attacks are not acceptable, in any of the project's spaces.

To report unacceptable behaviour, use GitHub's private security reporting on this repository (it reaches the maintainers privately), or contact a maintainer directly. Reports are handled confidentially.
